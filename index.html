How to use the workspace

Three simple steps
01

Connect a model

The included model loads automatically. To use another, choose Hosted model and enter its public model folder URL.

02

Choose an input

Use Camera for live analysis, or select Upload image to analyze a JPG, PNG, or WebP file.

The leading class and confidence scores update as the image is analyzed. Use them as guidance, and verify important decisions.

Camera access works on HTTPS or localhost. Image analysis runs locally in your browser after the model loads.
